**App name (30):** HGI Billing – GST Invoice

**Full description**

HGI Billing is a GST billing app for Indian small businesses, traders, distributors and manufacturers. It was built
for daily use at HARIGURU INDUSTRIES, so it is designed for real day-to-day billing.

• GST tax invoices with the correct CGST + SGST or IGST, auto-detected from the buyer's GSTIN and place of supply
• Per-item GST rates (GST 2.0 slabs), HSN/SAC, UQC units, discounts and freight handled as the law requires
• Bill of supply, credit notes, debit notes, SEZ and export (LUT) invoices
• Quotations / estimates that turn into an invoice with one tap
• Stock tracking with low-stock alerts
• Live compliance check while you type: GSTIN check digit, HSN digits, e-way bill limit, numbering and more
• Payments received, TDS, partial payments, overdue alerts and interest
• GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B summaries, HSN summary and a sales register for your CA
• Share invoice PDFs on WhatsApp or e-mail, or print through any Android printer
• UPI scan-to-pay QR on every invoice
• English, हिन्दी and ગુજરાતી app interface, plus dark mode
• Works fully offline. Your data stays on your phone, with no account and no ads
• PIN lock and one-tap backup

HGI Billing does not file returns or generate e-invoices / e-way bills on the government portal. Please verify the GST rate of each
product with your tax adviser.
