Privacy Policy — HGI Billing (Android)

Last updated: 29 October 2026 — applies from app version 8.1 (cloud sync).

HGI Billing is published by HARIGURU INDUSTRIES, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India ("HGI", "we").

1. Your account

You sign in with your Google account. From Google we receive your name, e-mail address and Google account ID. We use them only to identify your account and keep your data separate from every other user. We never receive your Google password.

2. The data you enter

You enter business data: company profile (including GSTIN, bank and UPI details), customers and buyers, products and stock, invoices, quotations, credit/debit notes and payments.

  • It is stored on your phone, in the app's private storage.
  • A copy is synced automatically to HGI's cloud storage so it can be restored when you reinstall the app or move to another phone. The copy is kept as a Google Sheets spreadsheet for your account in a Google Drive controlled by HARIGURU INDUSTRIES. Google LLC hosts it as our service provider (Google Cloud and Google Workspace/Drive).
  • Authorised HGI staff can open your spreadsheet — for example to help you with support or to recover data. We do not use your business data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires.

3. When data leaves the phone

  • Automatic sync to HGI's cloud storage (above), always over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • When you choose to share a PDF, CSV or backup file (WhatsApp, e-mail, Drive, …) or open a link (WhatsApp, UPI payment).

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. Android's own Google cloud backup of the app is switched off; the HGI sync replaces it.

4. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The app never holds a key to Drive or Sheets: only HGI's sync server, running on Google Cloud, can write to the spreadsheets, and it lets each signed-in account read and write only its own data. You can also protect the app with a PIN. Data on the phone is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your cloud copy while your account exists. You can delete it at any time:

  • in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account and cloud data, or
  • on the web: the account-deletion page linked from the app's Google Play listing (sign in with the same Google account), or
  • by e-mail to info@hariguru.com from the account's e-mail address.

Deletion removes the spreadsheet with your business data at once (if our storage moves it to the Drive trash first, it is erased from there within 30 days). We keep only a small marker (a random-looking account code and the deletion date, no business data) so that phones still signed in to the deleted account stop syncing.

6. Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.
