Delete your HGI Billing account

This deletes the cloud copy of your business data (company profile, customers, products, invoices, quotations, notes and payments) that HGI Billing keeps for your Google account. It cannot be undone. Data still on your phone is removed when the app next connects, or when you uninstall it.

You can also do this in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account and cloud data, or e-mail {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your account's address.
