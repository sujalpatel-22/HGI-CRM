Delete your HGI Billing account

This deletes your HGI Billing account and the business data stored for it in the cloud: company profile, customers, products, invoices, quotations, notes, invoice lines and payments. It cannot be undone. Data still on a phone is removed when the app next connects, or when you uninstall it.

You can also do this in the app: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account and cloud data, or e-mail {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
